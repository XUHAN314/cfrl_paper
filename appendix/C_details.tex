\section{Experimental details}
\label{app:details}

\paragraph{Base models.} The released-model rows of Table~\ref{tab:main} evaluate each released checkpoint with the task prompt and no task training: GPT-2 (124M), LLaDA-8B-Instruct (256 generated tokens), and ELF-B's OpenWebText checkpoint under the deployment chain.
ELF-B \citep{hu2026elf}: 105M-parameter transformer denoiser, frozen T5-small \citep{raffel2020t5} encoder embeddings ($d{=}512$), x-prediction with self-conditioning and training-time classifier-free guidance, PyTorch port of the released code. A denoiser call combines a prompt-conditioned and an unconditioned evaluation, $\hat x=\hat x_{\rm u}+\omega(\hat x_{\rm c}-\hat x_{\rm u})$, with guidance weight $\omega=2$ in every rollout and evaluation (Appendix~\ref{app:datasets}); the critic's copy is evaluated without guidance. Supervised fine-tuning uses the released recipe (AdamW, learning rate $2\times10^{-3}$ with warm-up, global batch 256--512, EMA of weights; EMA decay 0.99 for the short Game24 run and 0.999 otherwise). The Countdown base is epoch 60 of a 100-epoch run; Sudoku uses the converged model (76 epochs on 400k puzzles); K\&K the epoch-4 checkpoint of a 40-epoch schedule stopped at epoch 9; Game24 the epoch-20 checkpoint of a 40-epoch run initialised from the Countdown model.

\paragraph{Critic.}
The critic reuses the denoiser as a feature extractor: a copy of the whole network (the features $f_i$ of Section~\ref{sec:critic}), fine-tuned at $10^{-5}$ during pre-training and frozen during RL, reads the re-noised state $y_k$ at its query time $\tilde t_k$ together with the estimate $\hat x_{k-1}$ it was formed from, exactly as the sampler's denoiser call does, and a per-position MLP head ($d{+}1\to256\to64\to1$, GELU; the extra input is $\tilde t_k$) produces $R_{k,i}$; the value is the mean of $R_{k,i}$ over generated positions. The scalar critic is the same network with the head applied to the mean of the features (head after the mean, eq.~\ref{eq:critic1d}), pre-trained on the same rollouts and stored steps with the same epoch budget and selection (kept epochs 10, 3, 10 and 6 on Countdown, Sudoku, K\&K and Game24; the 2D heads keep epochs 9, 1, 10 and 6) and trained jointly with the same settings; the two rows of Table~\ref{tab:main} differ in nothing else. Pre-training: rollouts of the base policy with states stored at several flow steps and the verifier outcome, BCE on $\mathrm{sig}(V_\phi)$, batch 64, and the epoch with the best held-out field score kept (Table~\ref{tab:critic_pretrain}); the field score is the mean over the stored steps of half the Pearson correlation between the minimum per-position value and the outcome plus half the within-prompt rank correlation between the pooled value and the outcome (for the scalar critic, which has no per-position values, the pooled value replaces the minimum). The states are sampled under the deployment sampler except on Countdown, where states of the deployment sampler at the early and middle steps carry almost no information about the outcome (held-out AUC about $0.6$ for $k\le24$ of 64) and the critic is pre-trained on states of the cheap $\gamma4$@16 sampler instead; the choice is made by the held-out field score.\begin{table}[H]\centering\small

\paragraph{\methodSVGScalar{} and \methodSVGTwoD{} (Algorithm~\ref{alg:jpc}).}
32 prompts per iteration, deployment-sampler rollouts without gradients, one random step in the window $\{8,\dots,40\}$ of 64 ($\{4,\dots,20\}$ of 32) recomputed with gradients, loss $-V_\phi(s_k)$ averaged over prompts, AdamW on the actor with gradient clipping at 1.0, learning rate $10^{-6}$ decayed on a cosine to $10^{-7}$ over the run (1500 iterations on Countdown and Game24, 1000 on Sudoku and K\&K). Replay buffer of 16{,}384 states, filled each iteration from 32 fresh prompts rolled out to the end of the chain (separately from the actor's rollouts) with the state at the top of the window ($k{=}40$ of 64; $20$ of 32) and its outcome; 2 critic steps per iteration on minibatches of 64, updating the head at learning rate $10^{-4}$ with the copy of the denoiser frozen, loss~(\ref{eq:critic2d_loss}); every 10 iterations the pooled value's AUC (area under the ROC curve) against the outcome is computed on the newest 256 buffer states, and if it is below $0.9$ the critic takes 8 extra steps. In the symbols of Algorithm~\ref{alg:jpc}: $T{=}1500$ (Countdown, Game24) or $1000$ (Sudoku, K\&K), $B{=}32$, $B'{=}64$, $N_{\mathcal B}{=}16{,}384$, $m_{\rm c}{=}2$, $m'_{\rm c}{=}8$, $n_q{=}10$, $N_q{=}256$, $a_0{=}0.9$, $\mathcal W=\{8,\dots,40\}$ of 64 ($\{4,\dots,20\}$ of 32), $\eta_\theta=10^{-6}\to10^{-7}$, $\eta_\phi=10^{-4}$. Checkpoints are saved every 250 iterations (GRPO: every 100) and evaluated as described under \emph{Evaluation} below. The scalar critic has no per-position readings and minimises the first term of~(\ref{eq:critic2d_loss}) alone (Section~\ref{sec:critic}). The cheap-chain row of Table~\ref{tab:needs} and its reference use 1000 iterations at a constant learning rate of $10^{-6}$, a buffer of the states at steps $8,16,24,32,40$ of 64, the pooled loss term only and no AUC check. Runs take 45--90 minutes for 1000 iterations on one H200 (2--3 hours on an L40).

\paragraph{\methodGRPO{}.}
Group size 8, 16 prompts per iteration (128 rollouts), $\gamma4$@16 rollouts, uniform credit over steps and positions, group-normalised advantage, no KL, no clipping (on-policy REINFORCE form; the clipped per-position variant is in Tables~\ref{tab:sf_variants} and~\ref{tab:grpo_variants}), learning rate $3\times10^{-6}$, 8 transitions per update, 600 iterations, checkpoints every 100 iterations, evaluated as under \emph{Evaluation} below.

\paragraph{AR control.}
GPT-2 (124M) \citep{radford2019gpt2} is fine-tuned on each task's training set (batch 128, learning rate $3\times10^{-4}$, warm-up 500; 10 epochs, 20 on Sudoku), decoded greedily and scored by the same verifiers as the flow model: Countdown 44.2\% (epoch 8), Sudoku 1.2\% (23 of 2{,}000 puzzles at epoch 20), K\&K 92.9\% (epoch 10), Game24 26.9\% (epoch 2, initialised from the Countdown-fine-tuned weights, as the flow model is). Token-level GRPO/PPO uses 32 prompts $\times$ 8 samples per iteration, clip 0.2, no KL, 2 inner epochs, 600 iterations evaluated every 50, learning rate $10^{-5}$ on Countdown, Sudoku and Game24 and $3\times10^{-6}$ on K\&K, where the supervised policy is already sharp; dropout is disabled during RL, as in standard PPO implementations, so that the two forward passes that form the ratio agree; the log importance ratio is clamped to $\pm10$ as a numerical safeguard. Curves: Appendix~\ref{app:ar_curves}.

\paragraph{Amortisation loop.}
The loop of Table~\ref{tab:loop} runs 4 rounds on Countdown and 5 on K\&K (Table~\ref{tab:loop_full} adds one Sudoku round; Game24 has no pool of unlabelled problems, and the rounds run on re-orderings of its training sets are reported only as a check in Appendix~\ref{app:g24_loop}). In each round the current policy samples its pool at the deployment sampler (Countdown: a disjoint unlabelled pool of 50k problems, $n{=}4$ samples per problem; Game24, for the check only: the 4{,}693 other orderings of the training sets, $n{=}8$; K\&K: the 50k training problems in round 1 and a fresh 50k pool in every later round, $n{=}2$; Sudoku: 50k fresh puzzles, $n{=}2$), verifier-accepted samples are kept with at most four (Countdown, Game24) or one (unique-solution tasks) per problem, and the policy is fine-tuned on them with the supervised recipe (20 epochs on Countdown, 8 elsewhere) from the previous round's final checkpoint; the tables report the final checkpoint of the last round. Starting policies: a 2D-critic checkpoint from the runs of Table~\ref{tab:main}, the SFT base, and a GRPO checkpoint. The gold-label control starts from the 2D-critic policy and fine-tunes, with the same rounds and schedule, on the gold labels of the same pools instead of the policy's samples (one gold derivation per problem; on K\&K the round-1 pool is the 50k training problems with their labels). On Countdown, rounds 3--4 of the 2D-policy loop sample the pool with the critic-guided sampler of Appendix~\ref{app:loop}: the cheap chain $\gamma4$@16, $n_{\rm g}{=}4$ chains per prompt pruned at $k_p{=}8$ by the score~(\ref{eq:minscore}), and the push~(\ref{eq:guide}) with $\kappa{=}5$ at every step, all with the jointly trained 2D critic of the starting policy; this restores the pool's coverage (Appendix~\ref{app:cd_loop}). The controls have no critic and sample plainly in every round.

\paragraph{Evaluation.}\label{app:seeds}
Every number is one evaluation of the held-out prompts (2{,}000 per task, 1{,}601 for Game24) at classifier-free guidance 2. The held-out prompts are split by index into a validation half (even indices) and a test half (odd indices): checkpoints are selected by their validation accuracy at the deployment sampler (GRPO: every 100 of 600 iterations; the SVG variants: every 250; LLaDA: every 960 inner steps; ties to the earlier checkpoint) and every reported accuracy is measured on the test half.

\paragraph{Rollout budgets.}
GRPO: 16 prompts $\times$ 8 samples $=128$ rollouts per iteration under $\gamma4$@16 (16 denoiser calls each), 600 iterations, about $1.2$M denoiser calls per run. Algorithm~\ref{alg:jpc}: 32 actor rollouts and 32 buffer rollouts per iteration under the deployment chain (64 calls each; 32 on Sudoku), 1{,}000--1{,}500 iterations, about $4$--$6$M denoiser calls per run ($2$M on Sudoku) plus one differentiable call per actor rollout; GRPO is thus the cheaper of the two by three to five times in denoiser calls (under two on Sudoku), and it gains little under either chain (Appendix~\ref{app:grpo_chain}). GPT-2's token-level GRPO/PPO: 32 prompts $\times$ 8 samples per iteration, 600 iterations.

\paragraph{Compute.}
The campaign used about 300 GPU-hours on A100 (40/80 GB), H200 and L40 nodes; a single pathwise run costs about one GPU-hour on an H200, a loop round 15--40 minutes.
