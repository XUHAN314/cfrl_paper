\section{Experimental details}
\label{app:details}

\paragraph{Base models.}
ELF-B \citep{hu2026elf}: 105M-parameter transformer denoiser, frozen T5-small \citep{raffel2020t5} encoder embeddings ($d{=}512$), x-prediction with self-conditioning and training-time classifier-free guidance, PyTorch port of the released code. Supervised fine-tuning uses the released recipe (AdamW, learning rate $2\times10^{-3}$ with warm-up, global batch 256--512, EMA of weights; EMA decay 0.99 for the short Game24 run and 0.999 otherwise). The Countdown base is epoch 60 of a 100-epoch run; Sudoku uses the converged model (76 epochs on 400k puzzles); K\&K the epoch-4 checkpoint of a 40-epoch schedule stopped at epoch 9; Game24 the epoch-20 checkpoint of a 40-epoch run initialised from the Countdown model.

\paragraph{Critic.}
The critic reuses the denoiser architecture as a feature extractor (a copy of the trunk, fine-tuned at $10^{-5}$), takes the noisy state $y$, the time $t$ and the self-conditioning prediction $\hat x$ as input, and applies a per-position MLP head ($d{+}1\to256\to64\to1$, GELU) to produce $R_i$; the value is the mean of $R_i$ over generated positions (the scalar critic applies the same head to the mean of the features). Pre-training: rollouts of the base policy with states stored at several flow steps and the verifier outcome, BCE on $\sigma(V_\phi)$, batch 64, and the epoch with the best held-out field score kept (Table~\ref{tab:critic_pretrain}). The states are sampled under the deployment sampler except on Countdown, where states of the deployment sampler at the early and middle steps carry almost no information about the outcome (held-out AUC $\le0.6$ for $k\le24$ of 64) and the critic is pre-trained on states of the cheap $\gamma4$@16 sampler instead; the choice is made by the held-out field score. K\&K is the task where the pre-training budget matters (\S\ref{sec:ablations}).

\paragraph{\jpc{} (Algorithm~\ref{alg:jpc}).}
32 prompts per iteration, deployment-sampler rollouts without gradients, one random step in the window $[8,40]$ of 64 ($[4,20]$ of 32) recomputed with gradients, loss $-V_\phi(y_k)$ averaged over prompts, AdamW on the actor with gradient clipping at 1.0, learning rate $10^{-6}$ decayed on a cosine to $10^{-7}$ over the run (1500 iterations on Countdown and Game24, 1000 on Sudoku and K\&K). Replay buffer of 16{,}384 states holding, for every rollout, the states at five evenly spaced steps of the window ($8,16,24,32,40$ of 64; $4,8,12,16,20$ of 32) with their own time input; 2 critic steps per iteration on minibatches of 64 at learning rate $10^{-5}$, loss = mean of the pooled BCE and the per-position BCE; every 10 iterations the pooled value's AUC against the outcome is computed on the newest 256 buffer states of each stored step, and if the minimum is below $0.9$ the critic takes 8 extra steps. Checkpoints every 250 iterations are evaluated on the full held-out set; the reported checkpoint is the held-out peak. The scalar-critic rows of Table~\ref{tab:main} use the earlier variant of the recipe under which the scalar critic learns at all: $3\times10^{-6}$ for 500 iterations followed by $10^{-6}$ for 250 with actor and critic resumed, buffer states at the top of the window only, pooled BCE, 4{,}096 buffer entries. Runs take 45--90 minutes for 1000 iterations on one H200 (2--3 hours on an L40).

\paragraph{GRPO.}
Group size 8, 16 prompts per iteration (128 rollouts), $\gamma4$@16 rollouts, uniform credit over steps and positions, group-normalised advantage, no KL, no clipping (on-policy REINFORCE form; the clipped per-position variant is in Table~\ref{tab:sf_variants}), learning rate $3\times10^{-6}$, 8 transitions per update, 600 iterations, checkpoints every 100 evaluated on the held-out set with the peak reported. Three seeds per task.

\paragraph{AR control.}
GPT-2 (124M) fine-tuned on the Countdown training set for 10 epochs (batch 128, learning rate $3\times10^{-4}$, warm-up 500) reaches 44.2\% on the 2{,}000 held-out problems with greedy decoding; token-level GRPO/PPO with 32 prompts $\times$ 8 samples, clip 0.2, no KL, 2 inner epochs and learning rate $10^{-5}$ reaches 53.9\% at iteration 550 (evaluated every 50 iterations). For Game24 the same GPT-2 (initialised from the Countdown-fine-tuned weights, as the flow model is) reaches 26.9\% on the set-split evaluation after SFT and 32.2\% after 600 RL iterations.

\paragraph{Amortisation loop.}
Every loop starts from the best 2D-critic seed of Table~\ref{tab:main} (Countdown $47.5$, Sudoku $70.7$, K\&K $79.5$, Game24 $59.3$). Pool sampling uses the deployment sampler on training-side problems (Countdown: a disjoint unlabelled pool of 50k problems, 4 seeds; Game24: the 4{,}693 pool orderings, 8 seeds; K\&K: 50k training problems in round 1 and a fresh 50k pool in each later round, 2 seeds; Sudoku: 50k fresh puzzles, 2 seeds), keeps verifier-accepted samples with at most four (Countdown, Game24) or one (unique-solution tasks) per problem, and fine-tunes with the supervised recipe from the current policy (20 epochs on Countdown, 8 elsewhere); each round starts from the previous round's checkpoint (the final one on Countdown and K\&K, the best held-out one on Game24). On Countdown, rounds 3--4 sample the pool at $\gamma4$@16 with best-of-4 pruning at step 8 and gradient guidance by the jointly trained 2D critic of the starting policy, which restores the pool's coverage (Appendix~\ref{app:cd_loop}). The K\&K gold-label control fine-tunes on gold labels of the same fresh pools with the same schedule.

\paragraph{Evaluation and seeds.}
Every number is one evaluation of 2{,}000 held-out prompts (1{,}601 for Game24) at classifier-free guidance 2 with a fixed sampler seed; cross-GPU jitter of the same checkpoint is $\approx\pm0.3$ points. Seeds change the rollout noise and prompt order. \emph{Number of runs behind Table~\ref{tab:main}.} Every ELF-B RL row is a mean over three seeds of the per-seed peak over all evaluated checkpoints (GRPO: every 100 of 600 iterations; \jpc{}: every 250) at the deployment sampler, with the other samplers read at the same checkpoint (ties resolved to the earlier checkpoint). GRPO: Countdown $43.1/42.8/43.3$, Sudoku $70.1/72.8/66.2$, K\&K $70.2/70.0/74.6$, Game24 $46.8/48.6/47.1$. \jpc{} scalar critic: Countdown $51.2/49.2/44.3$, Sudoku $53.1/58.1/63.3$ (every seed below the base, collapsing by 500 iterations), K\&K $69.5/72.0/72.0$, Game24 $55.2/43.7/50.6$. \jpc{} 2D critic (Algorithm~\ref{alg:jpc}): Countdown $47.2/46.0/47.5$, Sudoku $69.5/69.7/70.7$, K\&K $79.5/79.1/79.0$, Game24 $59.3/58.5/58.8$. Every loop row, the controls, the GPT-2 rows and the LLaDA rows are single runs. Per-seed values at all four samplers are in Appendix~\ref{app:results}.

\paragraph{Compute.}
The campaign used about 300 GPU-hours on A100 (40/80 GB), H200 and L40 nodes; a single \jpc{} run costs about one GPU-hour on an H200, a loop round 15--40 minutes.
