\section{Experimental details}
\label{app:details}

\paragraph{Base models.}
ELF-B \citep{hu2026elf}: 105M-parameter transformer denoiser, frozen T5-small \citep{raffel2020t5} encoder embeddings ($d{=}512$), x-prediction with self-conditioning and training-time classifier-free guidance, PyTorch port of the released code. Supervised fine-tuning uses the released recipe (AdamW, learning rate $2\times10^{-3}$ with warm-up, global batch 256--512, EMA of weights; EMA decay 0.99 for the short Game24 run and 0.999 otherwise). The Countdown base is epoch 60 of a 100-epoch run; Sudoku uses the converged model (76 epochs on 400k puzzles); K\&K the epoch-4 checkpoint of a 40-epoch schedule stopped at epoch 9; Game24 the epoch-20 checkpoint of a 40-epoch run initialised from the Countdown model.

\paragraph{Critic.}
The critic reuses the denoiser as a feature extractor: a copy of the whole network (\ref{eq:feat}), fine-tuned at $10^{-5}$ during pre-training and frozen during RL, reads the re-noised state $y_k$ at its query time $\tilde t_k$ together with the estimate $\hat x_{k-1}$ it was formed from, exactly as the sampler's denoiser call does, and a per-position MLP head ($d{+}1\to256\to64\to1$, GELU; the extra input is $\tilde t_k$) produces $R_{k,i}$; the value is the mean of $R_{k,i}$ over generated positions. The scalar critic is the same network with the head applied to the mean of the features (pool-then-head), pre-trained on the same rollouts and stored steps with the same epoch budget and selection (kept epochs 10, 3, 10 and 6 on Countdown, Sudoku, K\&K and Game24; the 2D heads keep epochs 9, 1, 10 and 6) and trained jointly with the same settings; the two rows of Table~\ref{tab:main} differ in nothing else. Pre-training: rollouts of the base policy with states stored at several flow steps and the verifier outcome, BCE on $\mathrm{sig}(V_\phi)$, batch 64, and the epoch with the best held-out field score kept (Table~\ref{tab:critic_pretrain}); the field score is the mean over the stored steps of half the Pearson correlation between the minimum per-position value and the outcome plus half the within-prompt rank correlation between the pooled value and the outcome. The states are sampled under the deployment sampler except on Countdown, where states of the deployment sampler at the early and middle steps carry almost no information about the outcome (held-out AUC $\le0.6$ for $k\le24$ of 64) and the critic is pre-trained on states of the cheap $\gamma4$@16 sampler instead; the choice is made by the held-out field score. K\&K is the task where the pre-training budget matters (\S\ref{sec:ablations}).

\paragraph{\jpc{} (Algorithm~\ref{alg:jpc}).}
32 prompts per iteration, deployment-sampler rollouts without gradients, one random step in the window $[8,40]$ of 64 ($[4,20]$ of 32) recomputed with gradients, loss $-V_\phi(s_k)$ averaged over prompts, AdamW on the actor with gradient clipping at 1.0, learning rate $10^{-6}$ decayed on a cosine to $10^{-7}$ over the run (1500 iterations on Countdown and Game24, 1000 on Sudoku and K\&K). Replay buffer of 16{,}384 states, filled each iteration from 32 fresh prompts rolled out to the end of the chain (separately from the actor's rollouts) with the state at the top of the window ($k{=}40$ of 64; $20$ of 32) and its outcome; 2 critic steps per iteration on minibatches of 64, updating the head at learning rate $10^{-4}$ with the copy of the denoiser frozen, loss~(\ref{eq:critic2d_loss}); every 10 iterations the pooled value's AUC against the outcome is computed on the newest 256 buffer states, and if it is below $0.9$ the critic takes 8 extra steps. In the symbols of Algorithm~\ref{alg:jpc}: $B{=}32$, $B'{=}64$, $N_{\mathcal B}{=}16{,}384$, $m_{\rm c}{=}2$, $m'_{\rm c}{=}8$, $n_q{=}10$, $N_q{=}256$, $a_0{=}0.9$, $\mathcal W=[8,40]$ of 64 ($[4,20]$ of 32), $\eta_\theta=10^{-6}\to10^{-7}$, $\eta_\phi=10^{-4}$. (An earlier version of the recipe stored five evenly spaced steps of the window; \S\ref{sec:ablations} reports the comparison.) Checkpoints every 250 iterations are evaluated on the full held-out set and the reported checkpoint is the held-out peak; selection uses held-out prompts because the critic's value on training rollouts is the actor's objective and not an independent measure of success, and the same 2{,}000 prompts therefore serve for selection and for reporting (the peak is one of 4--6 checkpoints per run; GRPO is selected the same way over its 6 checkpoints). With the scalar critic the per-position BCE has a single position and the two loss terms coincide. \emph{Recipe versions behind the ablations of Table~\ref{tab:needs}.} Block A is this recipe. Block B differs in the replay buffer only, which holds the states at five evenly spaced steps of the window ($8,16,24,32,40$ of 64) instead of the top; its three reference seeds reach $59.3/58.5/58.8$ on Game24. Block C is the first joint-training recipe: the same five-step buffer, the pooled loss term only, no AUC check, a constant learning rate of $10^{-6}$ and 1000 iterations; its three reference seeds are the mean row of that block. Runs take 45--90 minutes for 1000 iterations on one H200 (2--3 hours on an L40).

\paragraph{GRPO.}
Group size 8, 16 prompts per iteration (128 rollouts), $\gamma4$@16 rollouts, uniform credit over steps and positions, group-normalised advantage, no KL, no clipping (on-policy REINFORCE form; the clipped per-position variant is in Table~\ref{tab:sf_variants}), learning rate $3\times10^{-6}$, 8 transitions per update, 600 iterations, checkpoints every 100 evaluated on the held-out set with the peak reported. Three seeds per task.

\paragraph{AR control.}
GPT-2 (124M) is fine-tuned on each task's training set (batch 128, learning rate $3\times10^{-4}$, warm-up 500; 10 epochs, 20 on Sudoku), decoded greedily and scored by the same verifiers as the flow model: Countdown 44.2\% (epoch 8), Sudoku 1.2\% (23 of 2{,}000 puzzles at epoch 20), K\&K 92.9\% (epoch 10), Game24 26.9\% (epoch 2, initialised from the Countdown-fine-tuned weights, as the flow model is). Token-level GRPO/PPO uses 32 prompts $\times$ 8 samples per iteration, clip 0.2, no KL, 2 inner epochs, 600 iterations evaluated every 50, learning rate $10^{-5}$ on Countdown, Sudoku and Game24 and $3\times10^{-6}$ on K\&K, where the supervised policy is already sharp and $10^{-5}$ collapses it within 50 iterations; the log importance ratio is clamped to $\pm10$ as a numerical safeguard, which never binds on the other tasks. Best checkpoints: 53.9 (iteration 550), 1.4 (100), 93.2 (150), 32.2 (600); Appendix~\ref{app:ar_curves} has the curves.

\paragraph{Amortisation loop.}
Every loop row of Tables~\ref{tab:main} and~\ref{tab:ode} runs the same procedure: a fixed number of rounds per task (Countdown 4, Sudoku 1, K\&K 5, Game24 4). In each round the current policy samples its pool at the deployment sampler (Countdown: a disjoint unlabelled pool of 50k problems, 4 seeds; Game24: the 4{,}693 pool orderings, 8 seeds; K\&K: the 50k training problems in round 1 and a fresh 50k pool in every later round, 2 seeds; Sudoku: 50k fresh puzzles, 2 seeds), verifier-accepted samples are kept with at most four (Countdown, Game24) or one (unique-solution tasks) per problem, and the policy is fine-tuned on them with the supervised recipe (20 epochs on Countdown, 8 elsewhere) from the previous round's final checkpoint; the tables report the final checkpoint of the last round. Starting policies: the best 2D-critic seed of Table~\ref{tab:main} (Countdown $50.4$, Sudoku $71.3$, K\&K $80.0$, Game24 $59.3$), the SFT base, and the best GRPO seed ($43.3$, $72.8$, $74.6$, $48.6$). The gold-label control starts from the 2D-critic policy and fine-tunes, with the same rounds and schedule, on the gold labels of the same pools instead of the policy's samples (one gold derivation per problem; on K\&K the round-1 pool is the 50k training problems with their labels). On Countdown, rounds 3--4 of the 2D-policy loop sample the pool with the critic-guided sampler of \S\ref{sec:loop}: the cheap chain $\gamma4$@16, $n_{\rm g}{=}4$ chains per prompt pruned at $k_p{=}8$ by the score~(\ref{eq:minscore}), and the push~(\ref{eq:guide}) with $\kappa{=}5$ at every step, all with the jointly trained 2D critic of the starting policy; this restores the pool's coverage (Appendix~\ref{app:cd_loop}). The controls have no critic and sample plainly in every round.

\paragraph{Evaluation and seeds.}
Every number is one evaluation of 2{,}000 held-out prompts (1{,}601 for Game24) at classifier-free guidance 2 with a fixed sampler seed; cross-GPU jitter of the same checkpoint is $\approx\pm0.3$ points. Seeds change the rollout noise and prompt order. \emph{Number of runs behind Table~\ref{tab:main}.} Every ELF-B RL row is a mean over three seeds of the per-seed peak over all evaluated checkpoints (GRPO: every 100 of 600 iterations; \jpc{}: every 250) at the deployment sampler, with the other samplers read at the same checkpoint (ties resolved to the earlier checkpoint). GRPO: Countdown $43.1/42.8/43.3$, Sudoku $70.1/72.8/66.2$, K\&K $70.2/70.0/74.6$, Game24 $46.8/48.6/47.1$. \jpc{} scalar critic: Countdown $48.5/47.4/48.6$, Sudoku $65.5/67.3/66.0$ (every seed peaks at 250 iterations and declines to $62$--$64$), K\&K $77.8/77.1/78.0$, Game24 $50.9/52.5/50.7$. \jpc{} 2D critic (Algorithm~\ref{alg:jpc}): Countdown $50.1/50.2/50.4$, Sudoku $70.8/71.3/71.0$, K\&K $79.7/79.2/80.0$, Game24 $58.2/59.3/58.9$. Every loop row, the controls, the GPT-2 rows and the LLaDA rows are single runs. Appendix~\ref{app:results} gives the seed means at all four samplers.

\paragraph{Compute.}
The campaign used about 300 GPU-hours on A100 (40/80 GB), H200 and L40 nodes; a single \jpc{} run costs about one GPU-hour on an H200, a loop round 15--40 minutes.
