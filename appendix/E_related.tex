\section{Related work}
\label{app:related}
\paragraph{Actor-critic control.}
Stochastic value gradients \citep{heess2015svg} differentiate a learned value through a reparameterised policy and a transition model; DDPG and SAC \citep{lillicrap2015ddpg,haarnoja2018sac} are the model-free cases. The SVG variants of \method{} are this pattern on a generative sampler whose transition model is the sampler itself, with a value that is a success probability fitted to verifier rewards and, in \methodSVGTwoD{}, a reading per position.

\paragraph{Self-training with verifiers.}
STaR, RFT, ReST-EM and related work \citep{zelikman2022star,yuan2023rft,singh2023restem,huang2022selfimprove} fine-tune a model on its own verified generations; expert iteration \citep{anthony2017exit} alternates a stronger search-based policy with distillation. The amortization loop is this pattern with an RL policy as the sampler and, in its guided form, with the RL critic as the search heuristic; the controls in Appendix~\ref{app:loop_full} separate what the starting policy contributes from what the rounds contribute. DAV \citep{lee2026dav} casts diffusion alignment as variational expectation-maximisation; a round of Algorithm~\ref{alg:loop} can be read as the M-step of that view for one prompt, with the RL policy as the sampler.

\paragraph{Tasks.}
Countdown and Game24 follow \citet{gandhi2024sos} and \citet{yao2023tot}; Sudoku follows \citet{shah2024sudoku}; Knights-and-Knaves follows \citet{xie2024kk}.
