\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}
Continuous-state language models go back to Diffusion-LM \citep{li2022diffusionlm}; ELF \citep{hu2026elf} removes intermediate discretisation with flow matching \citep{lipman2022flow,liu2022rectified,albergo2023stochastic} in a frozen embedding space. Masked diffusion language models \citep{lou2023sedd,sahoo2024mdlm,nie2025llada} have an RL literature built on likelihood surrogates \citep{zhao2025d1,zhu2025llada15}, none of which applies to a differentiable chain. RL for image diffusion and flows splits into score-function methods \citep{black2023ddpo,fan2023dpok,liu2025flowgrpo,xue2025dancegrpo,li2025mixgrpo}, likelihood-surrogate policy gradients \citep{mcallister2025fpo}, and reward back-propagation through a fixed differentiable reward, through one denoising step \citep[ReFL]{xu2023imagereward}, a truncated window \citep{clark2023draft}, or the full chain \citep{prabhudesai2023alignprop,domingoenrich2024adjoint}; our update matches ReFL's single-step depth, but its gradient comes from a jointly trained value of the noisy state rather than a frozen reward on a predicted clean sample, which makes the single step a per-transition estimate rather than a truncation (Appendix~\ref{app:pw_derivation}) and removes the exploitation a frozen reward invites (\S\ref{sec:exp_critic}); the critic is learned from a non-differentiable verifier, as in actor-critic control \citep{lillicrap2015ddpg,haarnoja2018sac}. AR post-training with verifiable rewards \citep{ouyang2022instructgpt,shao2024deepseekmath,deepseek2025r1,yu2025dapo,zheng2025gspo} is the estimator we port; the amortisation loop is rejection-sampling fine-tuning \citep{yuan2023rft,singh2023restem,zelikman2022star,huang2022selfimprove,anthony2017exit} with an RL policy as sampler (Appendix~\ref{app:related}). Masked diffusion models instead generate through latent denoising trajectories. Although an observed stochastic trajectory may have tractable transition probabilities, the marginal likelihood of its final sequence requires summing over possible trajectories and is generally intractable. Existing RL methods therefore approximate it using one-step mean-field likelihoods \citep{zhao2025d1} or variational bounds \citep{zhu2025llada15}. More recent process-aware methods assign step-specific advantages along the authentic denoising trajectory \citep{shao2026papo}, but each advantage remains shared across the token updates within that step.
