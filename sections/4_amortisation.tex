\section{Amortising the RL policy into the model}
\label{sec:loop}
A pathwise-trained policy realizes its largest gains under the sampler used during RL, and these gains may not transfer fully to cheaper samplers. We amortise them into the model through iterative verified self-training. In each round, the current policy generates multiple candidates for a pool of training problems under the RL sampler. The verifier filters these candidates, and the policy is fine-tuned on the accepted samples using the original supervised objective. Successive rounds begin from the preceding round's checkpoint, allowing verified improvements to accumulate in the model parameters. The 2D critic can also guide candidate generation. At an intermediate flow step, its value field ranks partial states for pruning and supplies a gradient for improving the retained states before generation continues. This guidance helps maintain the coverage of verified samples when unguided sampling saturates. The resulting procedure is a form of rejection-sampling fine-tuning \citep{yuan2023rft,singh2023restem,zelikman2022star} adapted to continuous-state language models, transferring gains obtained with the RL sampler to less expensive deployment samplers.
