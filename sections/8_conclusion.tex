\section{Conclusion}
We propose \method{}, RL post-training for continuous flow language models in three variants and an amortisation loop. \methodGRPO{} is the score-function estimator on the chain's exact density; \methodSVGScalar{} and \methodSVGTwoD{} are stochastic value gradients with one value per state, or per state and token position. \methodGRPO{} gains little: one reward bit is spread over the chain's noise coordinates, and its gradient agrees with itself no better than a zero-mean control, whereas GPT-2's gradient, with one score term per token decision, does. Differentiating the chain through a critic removes the noise factor. The critic must read the denoiser's estimate through a copy of the pre-trained denoiser and be refit after every actor step, or it is exploited. With one value per position the policy rises faster and further than with a scalar value, and exchanging the critics mid-training shows that the critic in charge decides. The loop carries the gain into a cheap chain on two of the three tasks with a pool. \emph{Limitations:} one 105M architecture, four synthetic tasks with exact verifiers, held-out checkpoint selection for every RL row, single runs for the ablations, critic variants and critic exchange, and \methodGRPO{} trained under a 16-step chain, although its gradient is measured under the deployment chain (Table~\ref{tab:main}).
