\section{Conclusion}
A flow language model's action is a path of noise coordinates, not a sequence of decisions; \method{} post-trains it in three variants. \methodGRPO{} ports the score-function estimator, which averages every reward bit over far more nuisance than in an AR model, and gains little. \methodSVGScalar{} differentiates the chain through a jointly trained critic, improves faster but flattens early, and on one task stays at the base. \methodSVGTwoD{}, our contribution, keeps the update improving with a value for every flow step and token position, pooled into the actor's objective and trained on the policy's own rollouts labelled by the verifier; it improves every task at every seed, and on two of the three tasks with an unlabelled pool its samples amortise into a cheap chain. \emph{Limitations:} one 105M architecture, four synthetic tasks with exact verifiers, and a held-out stopping point for the SVG variants.
