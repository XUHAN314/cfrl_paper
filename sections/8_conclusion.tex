\section{Conclusion}
We propose \method{}, RL post-training for continuous flow language models in three variants and an amortisation loop: \methodGRPO{}, the score-function estimator on the chain's exact density, and two stochastic-value-gradient variants, \methodSVGScalar{} with one value per state and \methodSVGTwoD{} with one per state and token position. \methodGRPO{} gains little: its gradient is spread over a path of noise coordinates that share one reward bit, and on rollouts of the base it agrees with itself no better than a zero-mean control. The SVG variants differentiate the chain through a jointly trained critic; the scalar critic's policy flattens or turns back early and on one task stays at the base. The 2D critic keeps the policy improving longer through the joint refit and peaks above the base at every seed, and the loop carries its verified samples into a cheap chain on two of the three tasks with a pool. \emph{Limitations:} one 105M architecture, four synthetic tasks with exact verifiers, held-out checkpoint selection for every RL row, and \methodGRPO{} trained under a cheap 16-step chain rather than the deployment chain.
