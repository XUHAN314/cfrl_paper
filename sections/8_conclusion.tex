\section{Conclusion}
A continuous flow language model's action is a path of noise coordinates, not a sequence of decisions; \method{} post-trains it at three levels. \methodGRPO{} ports the score-function estimator, which averages every reward bit over orders of magnitude more nuisance than in an AR model, and gains little. \methodSVGScalar{} differentiates the chain through a jointly trained critic, improves faster and flattens early. \methodSVGTwoD{}, our contribution, keeps the update improving with a value for every flow step and token position, pooled into the actor's objective and trained on the policy's own verified rollouts, and its samples amortise into cheap chains up to the pool's coverage. \emph{Limitations:} one 105M architecture, four synthetic tasks with exact verifiers, a held-out stopping point for the SVG variants, and a cheap-chain score-function baseline.
