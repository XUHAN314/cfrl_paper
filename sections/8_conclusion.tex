\section{Conclusion}
We propose \method{}, RL post-training for continuous flow language models in three variants: \methodGRPO{}, the score-function estimator on the chain's exact density, and two stochastic-value-gradient variants, \methodSVGScalar{} with one value per state and \methodSVGTwoD{} with one per state and token position. \methodGRPO{} gains little: its gradient, spread over a path of noise coordinates that share one reward bit, carries no signal we can detect. The SVG variants differentiate the chain through a jointly trained critic and improve substantially more, but the scalar critic flattens early and on one task stays at the base. The 2D critic keeps the policy improving through the joint refit on three tasks and peaks above the base on every task at every seed; on two of the three tasks with an unlabelled pool its verified samples amortise into a cheap chain. \emph{Limitations:} one 105M architecture, four synthetic tasks with exact verifiers, held-out checkpoint selection for every RL row, and \methodGRPO{} trained under the 16-step chain $\gamma4$@16 rather than the deployment chain.
