\section{Amortising the RL Policy Across Samplers}
\label{sec:loop}
Pathwise RL improves the policy most under its training sampler; and their gains may not transfer to cheaper samplers. We amortise these gains into the model parameters through verified self-training across all times $t$ rather than along one chain, so that cheaper samplers inherit them. The 2D critic can additionally guide candidate generation before verification.

\paragraph{Amortising loop.} Let $\pi_\theta(w\mid c)$ denote the response distribution induced by the chosen sampler. From a pool $\mathcal C$ of training prompts, we sample $n$ responses per prompt, retain deduplicated ones accepted by the verifier, and fine-tune the current policy on the resulting pairs with the original supervised objective of ELF \citep{hu2026elf} (Algorithm~\ref{alg:loop}):
\begin{align}
\mathcal A(\theta)
=
\left\{
(c,w^{(j)}):
c\in\mathcal C,\ 
w^{(j)}\sim\pi_\theta(\cdot\mid c),\
r(w^{(j)},c)=1
\right\},
\label{eq:accept}
\end{align}
Repeating the round from each updated policy transfers verified behavior into the denoiser parameters. This loop follows rejection-sampling self-training methods such as STaR, RFT, and ReST$^{\rm EM}$ \citep{zelikman2022star,yuan2023rft,singh2023restem}. Ideally, accepted responses sample the policy conditioned on success, and maximum-likelihood training on them performs an EM-style update that improves a tight lower bound on expected reward (Appendix~\ref{app:em}).


\paragraph{Critic-guided sampling.}
The 2D critic can improve pool coverage by ranking and guiding partial states. We score a state by its least confident position and move it toward higher critic value before the denoiser call:
\begin{equation}
u(s_k)
=
\min_{i\in\mathcal G}
\mathrm{sig}(R_{k,i}), \quad 
y_k
\leftarrow
y_k
+
\kappa\sigma_k\sqrt L\,
\frac{\nabla_yu(s_k)}
     {\|\nabla_yu(s_k)\|}.
\label{eq:minscore}
\end{equation}
For pruning, $n_{\rm g}$ chains are run to step $k_p$, after which only the highest-scoring chain continues. This guidance is applied directly to the sampled state at inference instead of training. Scaling by $\sigma_k$ adapts the update to the transition's noise level. Guidance requires additional critic backward passes and parallel prefixes, so we apply it with a cheaper sampling chain.
