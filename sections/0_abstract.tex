\begin{abstract}
Reinforcement learning (RL) has substantially improved the reasoning of autoregressive and discrete-diffusion language models.
Language models that generate continuous token embeddings are emerging as a strong alternative, but RL post-training for them remains understudied.
We propose \method{}, a post-training framework for continuous flow language models. Our key insight is that the continuous generation trajectory is differentiable:
a critic that predicts the verifier outcome from intermediate embeddings lets reward signals be backpropagated into the model,
giving gradient estimates with much lower variance than REINFORCE.
We further design a critic that predicts a value from every token representation and improves policy optimization.
Across four reasoning tasks, \method{} provides, to our knowledge, the first reliable RL post-training of a continuous flow language model.

\end{abstract}
