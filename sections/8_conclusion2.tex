\section{Conclusion}
We propose \method{}, an RL post-training framework for continuous flow language models with three variants: score-function GRPO, SVG with a scalar critic, and SVG with a 2D critic. GRPO yields modest gains because each reward is distributed across a high-dimensional noise trajectory. SVG exploits the differentiable flow chain and improves performance substantially, but the scalar critic eventually plateaus. Our 2D critic instead assigns values across flow steps and token positions, sustaining policy improvement through joint training. It substantially outperforms the base ELF model on all four tasks, while amortisation transfers its verified samples to cheaper samplers. \emph{Limitations:} our evaluation covers only the 105M architecture, four synthetic tasks with exact verifiers, held-out checkpoint selection for SVG, and GRPO trained primarily with the cheap chain.
